Mobile Arcade Game

Skydiver

A fast-paced arcade descent built around movement, timing, obstacle avoidance, and the challenge of staying in control as the world accelerates.

Explore the Game → All Projects

Project Overview

The challenge is the descent.

Skydiver is an arcade-style mobile game focused on navigating an increasingly demanding environment while collecting items and avoiding hazards.

The central idea behind Skydiver is straightforward: the player is falling, obstacles are approaching, and every decision must be made while the descent continues.

Movement, speed, collectible objects, and environmental hazards combine to create a gameplay loop centered on reaction time and spatial awareness.

Rather than relying on complicated controls, the project explores how a small collection of understandable mechanics can become challenging through their interaction and increasing pace.

Gameplay Design

Simple actions, increasing complexity.

The project explores several systems that work together to create an arcade gameplay experience.

Player Movement

Responsive Control

Player movement is central to avoiding danger and navigating the falling environment. The design emphasizes predictable responses to player input.

Obstacles

Environmental Hazards

Obstacles create the primary challenge by requiring the player to anticipate their positions and respond before a collision occurs.

Collectibles

Risk and Reward

Collectible objects provide opportunities for the player to pursue additional rewards while navigating hazards.

Increasing Speed

Difficulty Progression

Changes in descent speed increase the demands on reaction time, positioning, and decision-making during play.

Collision Behavior

Gameplay Rules

Different interactions between the player, obstacles, and collectibles produce changes in the game's current state.

Game State

Consistent Behavior

The game distinguishes normal movement, altered movement, and terminal conditions so different gameplay events can be handled consistently.

Technical Focus

Designing the systems behind the game.

Behind the arcade presentation, Skydiver provides an opportunity to explore common game-development responsibilities.

Game Loop

Frame-Based Updates

Gameplay objects are updated according to elapsed time, keeping movement and simulation behavior independent of a particular frame rate.

Object Responsibilities

Separation of Behavior

Player, obstacle, and collectible objects each manage behavior relevant to their own role, reducing the amount of unrelated logic held in a single class.

Rendering and Input

Presentation and Interaction

Visual rendering and player input are treated as distinct responsibilities from collision handling and core gameplay rules.

Design Philosophy

An engaging game does not need complicated rules.

Skydiver explores the appeal of games that are easy to understand but require practice to master.

The challenge comes from the interaction between movement, positioning, speed, and environmental hazards rather than from requiring the player to memorize extensive instructions.

The intended result is a game that rewards developing skill, learning from mistakes, and improving through repeated attempts.
