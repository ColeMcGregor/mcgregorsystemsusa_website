03 / After your inquiry

What happens after you reach out?

An inquiry begins a conversation, not a commitment to a project.

01

I review your inquiry.

I'll look over what you've described to get an initial understanding of the problem, the kind of work involved, and whether it's something McGregor Systems LLC may be able to help with.

02

We discuss the details.

If your project appears to be a potential fit, we'll discuss your goals, existing systems, constraints, and any important questions. We can communicate by email, phone, video call, or meet in person when practical, depending on your preference and the needs of the project.

You don't need to prepare a technical specification beforehand.

03

We determine an appropriate next step.

Depending on the project, that might mean gathering additional information, reviewing an existing system, preparing a proposal, or defining a Statement of Work.

04

We agree on the work before development begins.

If we decide to proceed, we'll establish the applicable scope, responsibilities, deliverables, pricing, and contractual terms before beginning the agreed development work.

Working together

Your involvement depends on the project.

Some work requires frequent feedback and decisions. Other engagements involve relatively little client involvement once the requirements are established. We'll discuss communication, review expectations, and responsibilities before work begins.
