01 / Information handling

Privacy Policy

This policy describes how McGregor Systems LLC collects, uses, and handles information submitted through this website.

1. Information we collect

When you submit a contact or project inquiry, the form may collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if provided
  • The type of service you are interested in
  • Project details and other information you choose to submit

Please do not include passwords, financial account details, Social Security numbers, medical records, or other sensitive information in an initial inquiry.

2. How we use information

Information submitted through the contact form is used to review and respond to inquiries, discuss possible projects, and communicate about requested services.

If a business relationship begins, related correspondence may also become part of the project's business records.

3. Third-party service providers

The website uses Formspree to receive and process contact form submissions. Information entered into a form is transmitted to Formspree for delivery.

Website hosting and email providers may also process technical or correspondence information as needed to provide their services.

Technology logos displayed in the homepage marquee are hosted locally as part of the website's static assets. Displaying these icons does not require requests to an external technology-logo delivery service.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

4. Technical information

When you visit this website, the hosting provider may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, browser information, requested resources, and request times.

This information may be needed for website delivery, security, and technical operation.

5. Cookies and analytics

The current website implementation does not include a dedicated advertising or visitor analytics system.

Service providers may use cookies or similar technologies as part of their own operations. If additional analytics or tracking services are introduced, this policy should be updated.

6. Information sharing

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website, deliver inquiries, and manage business correspondence.

McGregor Systems LLC does not sell or rent submitted inquiry information for third-party marketing.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

7. Information retention

Inquiry information may be retained as needed to respond to requests, manage potential or active business relationships, maintain business records, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods can vary based on the purpose of the information and the services involved.

8. Requests about your information

You may contact McGregor Systems LLC to ask about personal information submitted through this website or request its correction or deletion.

Requests will be reviewed subject to applicable law, verification needs, and business recordkeeping obligations.

9. Information security

We seek to use appropriate services and reasonable safeguards when handling website inquiries.

No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can guarantee complete security.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the website, services, or information handling practices change.

The date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.

02 / Website information

Website Terms & Pricing.

These notices explain the informational purpose of this website and how preliminary prices, project estimates, and scope-building tools should be interpreted.

1. General website information

This website describes the services, capabilities, and work of McGregor Systems LLC.

Service descriptions, example projects, pricing information, and other materials are intended for general informational purposes. They do not, by themselves, establish a client relationship, constitute an offer to perform services, or create a service agreement.

2. Preliminary pricing and estimates

Website estimates are not binding quotations.

Any price, cost range, estimated number of hours, starting rate, projected total, or other figure displayed or generated through this website is preliminary and informational only.

Such figures are not binding offers, guaranteed prices, or commitments by McGregor Systems LLC to complete work for the amount shown.

This includes estimates generated by interactive project planners, scope builders, pricing calculators, service selectors, and similar website tools.

An estimate may change following review of actual project requirements, technical constraints, and requested deliverables.

3. Factors affecting project pricing

Software development is not a uniform product or service. Two projects that appear similar may require substantially different amounts of work.

Actual pricing may depend on:

  • Project size, complexity, and technical requirements
  • The condition and architecture of existing software
  • Required features, integrations, and third-party dependencies
  • Data migration, compatibility, and modernization requirements
  • Security, accessibility, performance, and reliability needs
  • Hosting, infrastructure, licensing, and external service costs
  • Testing, documentation, maintenance, and ongoing support
  • Project schedules, delivery constraints, and requested changes

Some services may be suitable for standardized pricing. Others require discovery, technical review, or a more detailed scope of work before a responsible price can be established.

4. Interactive project planning tools

The website may provide interactive tools that help visitors describe projects, select desired features, explore service options, and develop a preliminary scope.

These tools assist early planning. They cannot account for every technical, operational, or business consideration involved in a software project.

A generated project summary does not guarantee the feasibility, availability, completion time, or final price of the proposed work.

Information submitted through a project planner or inquiry form will be handled according to the Privacy Policy.

5. Proposals and service agreements

Actual project pricing, deliverables, schedules, responsibilities, payment terms, and other material conditions are established through a separate written proposal, statement of work, or service agreement accepted by the parties.

If an accepted written agreement conflicts with general information appearing on this website, the applicable agreement governs the contracted work.

Submitting an inquiry, using the project scope builder, or viewing a preliminary estimate does not obligate a visitor to purchase services or McGregor Systems LLC to accept a project.

6. Information accuracy and changes

McGregor Systems LLC seeks to keep website information accurate and reasonably current.

Service descriptions, available technologies, pricing information, and other content may change over time.

Visitors should contact the company to confirm current service availability, pricing, or project-specific details.

7. Third-party names and materials

Product names, technology logos, trademarks, and third-party materials displayed on this website remain the property of their respective owners.

References to third-party technologies indicate tools or platforms relevant to the company's work. They do not imply sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or partnership unless explicitly stated.

Additional details are available in Credits & Attributions .
