01 / Background

Technical education and professional experience.

The company's engineering work draws on formal computer science education, software development projects, and experience maintaining systems that other people depend on.

The founder holds a Bachelor of Science in Software Development from Siena College and an Associate of Science in Computer Science from Hudson Valley Community College.

Before establishing the business, the founder also served in the United States Air Force, including supervisory and team leadership responsibilities. That background informs the company's approach to accountability, communication, and completing assigned work.
