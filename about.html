Meet the founder

Cole McGregor

Founder & Software Engineer

I'm the founder of McGregor Systems LLC and the engineer behind its software development work. I established the business to provide software engineering services directly to businesses and organizations, with an emphasis on understanding their needs and developing practical solutions.

My background includes software development, systems design, technical troubleshooting, and leadership experience. I enjoy working through challenging problems, learning new technologies, and finding ways to make software more useful to the people who depend on it.

Beyond providing contract software engineering services, I also established McGregor Systems LLC with the intention of developing and releasing original software products. That work takes place through Wise Raven Studios, the company's independent software and game-development studio. The goal is to create useful applications, educational tools, and games that can become products for the people who will use them.

01 / Background

A foundation in software and systems.

I came to software engineering with experience working in teams, leading people, troubleshooting technical issues, and solving practical problems. Formal education and development projects helped me build on that foundation and apply it to designing and maintaining software.

I studied computer science at Hudson Valley Community College before earning my Bachelor of Science in Software Development at Siena College. Through that education, I developed a foundation in software architecture, application development, and engineering principles.

Prior to my software engineering career, I served in the United States Air Force in technical and supervisory capacities. My responsibilities included troubleshooting, coordinating work, and leading personnel, providing experience that complements my technical education and development work.

03 / How we work

The values behind my work.

Technical knowledge matters, but so does how that knowledge is applied. These four values influence how I approach engineering decisions, communicate with clients, and work with others.

01

Honesty

I believe in being straightforward about what I know, what I don't know, and what a project requires. That includes discussing costs, limitations, and potential problems rather than making promises I can't support.

02

Curiosity

I'm naturally interested in learning how things work, exploring unfamiliar ideas, and understanding perspectives different from my own. That curiosity carries into my engineering work, where asking questions often reveals possibilities that weren't obvious at first.

03

Creativity

I enjoy creating things, exploring new ideas, and finding different ways to approach problems. Whether I'm developing something from scratch or improving what already exists, I value the freedom to experiment and find solutions that aren't always obvious.

04

Kindness

I believe in treating people with patience, respect, and understanding. Everyone brings different experiences and perspectives to the table, and I try to make collaboration a place where people feel comfortable asking questions, sharing ideas, and being heard.
